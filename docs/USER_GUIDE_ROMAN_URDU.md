# My Daily OS — Asaan User Guide (Roman Urdu)

Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jo pehli dafa My Daily OS istemal kar rahe hain. Koi mushkil technical lafz nahi — jahan koi English lafz aya hai, wahan sath us ka matlab bhi samjhaya gaya hai.

---

## 1. My Daily OS kya hai?

My Daily OS aap ki zindagi ka ek **personal system** hai — ek hi jagah jahan aap:

- Apne din ki **planning** kar sakte hain (aaj kya karna hai, kis waqt karna hai)
- Apni **routine** bana sakte hain (jo kaam roz repeat hotay hain)
- Paanch **namazon** ka record rakh sakte hain
- **Sehat** track kar sakte hain — pani, khana, walk, exercise, wazan, neend
- **Office** ki hazri aur kaam ka record
- **Freelancing** ke projects aur kamai
- **Learning** (seekhna) aur **habits** (aadatein)
- **Amdani aur kharche** (Finance)
- Aur phir **Reports** mein dekh sakte hain ke aap waqt ke sath behtar ho rahe hain ya nahi

Sab se ahem sawal jis ka jawab ye app deta hai:

> **"Aaj mujhe kya karna hai, kis waqt karna hai, aur main apni planning par kitna chal raha hoon?"**

Aap ka tamam data sirf **aap ke account** mein mehfooz rehta hai. Phone par jo mark karein ge, laptop par waisa hi dikhe ga — kyunki data asal mein server ke database mein save hota hai, sirf phone ki memory mein nahi.

## 2. Pehle din ki setup (sirf ek dafa)

App pehli dafa kholne par Dashboard par **"Set up My Daily OS"** wala card nazar aye ga. Us par click karne se **Set Up Guide (Welcome)** page khulta hai — ek checklist jis mein har step ke sath samjhaya gaya hai ke wo kya hai. Koi step pehle, koi baad mein — koi pabandi nahi. Jo step aap waqai kar lein ge, us par khud tick lag jayega.

Setup ke steps ye hain:

### Step 1: Profile aur basic settings
**Settings** page par jayein: apna sheher (City: Faisalabad), mulk (Country: Pakistan) aur Timezone (Asia/Karachi) check kar lein. Ye is liye zaroori hai kyunki namaz ke auqaat aur tamam gharhiyan (clock) inhi se chalti hain.

### Step 2: Office ka time
Settings → **Work** section mein:
- **Office start:** 5:00 PM (shaam 5 bajay)
- **Office end:** 2:00 AM (raat 2 bajay)
- **Working days:** jin dinon aap office jatay hain, un par tick kar dein (masalan Monday se Friday)

Note: Office ka time **aadhi raat ke baad** khatam ho (2:00 AM) to koi masla nahi — app isay sahi samajhta hai.

### Step 3: Salah settings
**Salah → Prayer Settings** mein check kar lein:
- Sheher aur coordinates (Faisalabad pehle se set hain)
- **Calculation method:** Karachi (ye Fajr aur Isha ke angle hain)
- **Asr method:** Hanafi

Ye do alag cheezein hain — Asr ke liye Hanafi tareeqa hai, aur Fajr/Isha ke liye Karachi convention. Ek ko dusre se mix na karein. Agar kisi masjid ke time se chand minute ka farq ho to **minute adjustments** mein har namaz ke liye + ya − minute add kar sakte hain.

### Step 4: Health targets
Settings → **Health targets** mein apne hadaf (targets) likhein:
- Roz ka pani: masalan 2500 ml (takreeban 10 glass)
- Roz ki walk: masalan 3 km
- Target wazan: masalan 70 kg
- Haftay mein exercise sessions: masalan 3

Ye sirf aap ki rehnumai ke liye hain — koi dabao nahi.

### Step 5: Notifications on karein
**Settings → Notifications** par jayein:
1. **"Enable push on this device"** dabayein — browser ijazat (permission) mangay ga, **Allow** kar dein
2. **"Send test notification"** dabayein — ek test notification aye ga, is se pata chal jayega ke system chal raha hai

Notifications ki tafseel neeche section 9 mein hai.

### Step 6: Theme (agar pasand ho)
Settings → **Appearance** mein teen options hain:
- **Dark:** asal gehra (dark) rang — raat ko ankhon ke liye asaan
- **Light:** roshan theme — din ki roshni mein saaf
- **System:** jo aap ke phone/computer ki apni setting ho, waisa hi

Sath mein **accent color** bhi chun sakte hain — ye wo rang hai jo buttons, links aur graphs mein nazar ata hai. Rang badalte hi poori app badal jati hai, aur ye choice aap ke account mein save rehti hai — har device par wohi dikhe ga.

## 3. Routine banana (app ka dil)

Ye samajhna sab se zaroori hai:

- **Routine** = wo plan jo **repeat** hota hai. Masalan roz subah walk, roz learning, roz office.
- **Task** = **ek din ka ek kaam**. Masalan "kal client ko call karni hai" — ye sirf ek dafa hai.
- **My Day** = routine ki **aaj wali copy** + aaj ke tasks, dono ek sath time ki tarteeb mein.

### Routine kese banayein?
1. Side menu mein **My Routine** kholein
2. Neeche wale form mein activity ka naam likhein — masalan "Morning walk"
3. **Planned start** aur **Planned end** likhein — masalan start 7:00 AM, end 7:30 AM
   - *Planned start ka matlab hai: aap kis waqt ye kaam shuru karne ka irada rakhte hain. End optional hai.*
4. **Repeat** chunein:
   - **Every day** — roz
   - **Weekdays (Monday to Friday)** — sirf kaam wale din
   - **Custom days…** — khud din tick kar dein (masalan sirf Saturday, Sunday — weekend wali activities ke liye)
   - **Every week / Every month** — haftawar ya mahana
5. **Add to routine** dabayein

Activities do groups mein dikhti hain: **Workday routine** (kaam wale din) aur **Days off & other** (chutti wale din).

### Yaad rakhein
- Routine badalne se **sirf aanay wale din** badalte hain. Guzray hue dinon ki history **kabhi nahi badalti**.
- Koi activity delete karne se us ki purani copies (jo din guzar chuke) mehfooz rehti hain.
- Office ke hours routine mein dalne ki zaroorat nahi — wo Settings ke Work section se khud Dashboard ki timeline par ajate hain.

## 4. Ek misaal wala poora din (subah se raat tak)

Faraz karein aap ka ek aam din kesa chalta hai, aur har qadam par app mein kya karna hai:

**Subah uthna**
- Dashboard kholein. Upar greeting, tareekh aur waqt nazar aye ga.
- **Next Up** card bataye ga ke ab agla kaam kya hai aur kis waqt hai.
- **Today's Timeline** mein poora din time ki tarteeb mein dikhe ga — namazein, routine activities, tasks aur office hours, sab ek hi list mein.

**Fajr**
- Namaz parhne ke baad Dashboard ki timeline mein Fajr ke sath wale chhote form se status chunein: **Waqt par** (waqt par parhi), **Late** (der se), **Qaza** (qaza ki) ya **Missed** ( reh gayi) — aur Save dabayein.
- Jo namaz abhi nahi parhi, use **Unmarked** hi rehne dein. App khud se kisi namaz ko "Missed" nahi kehta — ye faisla hamesha aap ka hai.

**Nashta**
- **Meals → Add Meal** — Breakfast chunein, khane ka naam likhein (masalan "Anda paratha"), Save.
- Calories likhna **lazmi nahi** hai — sirf tab likhein jab aap ko khud andaza ho. Ye aap ka apna andaza hota hai, app khud se koi number nahi banata.

**Pani**
- Jab glass piyein, Dashboard par **"Log Water +250 ml"** ka button dabayein — ek click, pani add. Ya Water page par +250 / +500 / +750 ml ke quick buttons hain.
- Dashboard par progress bar bataye ga ke aaj ke target (masalan 2500 ml) mein se kitna ho gaya.

**Learning session**
- Apni routine ke time par learning karein. Khatam karke **Learning** page par session add kar dein — topic, kitne minute, kis skill ke liye.

**Walk / Exercise**
- Walk ke baad **Health → Walking** mein distance (km) aur minute likh dein. Gym ya push-ups hon to **Exercise** mein add kar dein.

**Office — shaam 5 bajay se raat 2 bajay**
- Timeline mein office block apne time par "Now" highlight ho jayega.
- **Office** page par aaj ki hazri (attendance) mark kar dein — start time, end time (2:00 AM next day bilkul theek hai), break ke minute.

**Raat ka khana aur freelancing**
- Dinner Meals mein log kar dein.
- Freelancing ka kaam kiya ho to **Freelancing** mein project update kar dein; paise mile hon to **Income** mein add kar dein — client, platform (Fiverr/Upwork/Direct), gross, fees; net khud calculate ho jata hai. PKR aur USD dono chal sakte hain; dono ko milane ke liye app koi zabardasti rate nahi lagata — exchange rate aap khud dein ge jab chahiye ho.

**Sone se pehle — 2 minute ka review**
1. **Habits** page kholein — jo aadat aaj ki hai, us ke sath **"Done today"** dabayein
2. **Sleep** page par **Start sleep** dabayein (ya subah uth kar manual entry kar dein: kab soye, kab uthe — neend aadhi raat cross kare to bhi sahi record hoti hai, masalan subah 6 bajay so kar dopahar 2 bajay uthna bhi theek likha jata hai)
3. **Reports → Daily** kholein — poora din ek nazar mein: tasks kitne hue, namazein, pani, kharcha, sab

Bas. Kal subah Dashboard kholte hi kal ki planning pehle se wahan hogi — kyunki routine khud copy bana deta hai.

## 5. Tasks — My Day

- **Add Task:** naam saaf likhein — "Complete SEO client audit" jesa. **Date** aj ki pehle se selected hoti hai.
- **Planned start / Planned end:** kab shuru aur kab khatam karne ka irada hai. Agar kaam kisi bhi waqt ho sakta hai to dono khali chor dein — wo "Anytime" mein ajayega.
- **Deadline vs planned time ka farq:** planned time wo hai jab aap kaam **karein ge**. Agar koi cheez kisi din tak **lazmi khatam** honi hai, to details mein wo baat likh dein aur Date usi din ki rakh dein.
- **Repeat:** agar koi task bar bar hota hai to repeat chunein (har din / har haftay usi din / sirf weekdays / har mahina / custom din). Har din ki alag copy banti hai, purani history mehfooz rehti hai. Wese roz wale kaam ke liye **My Routine** zyada behtar jagah hai.
- Task par ye actions hain: **Complete** (ho gaya), **Reopen** (galti se complete ho gaya tha), **Move to tomorrow** (aj nahi ho sakta, kal par dal dein), **Duplicate** (waisa hi ek aur), **Edit**, aur **Delete**.
- **Delete karna:** Delete dabane par button pehle **"Confirm delete?"** mein badalta hai — dobara dabane par hi delete hota hai, taake galti se kuch na uray. Aur koi bhi button double-click karne se kaam do dafa nahi hota — pehle click ke baad button khud band (disable) ho jata hai jab tak kaam pura na ho.

## 6. Salah (Namaz)

- Har roz paanch namazon ke **calculated auqaat** nazar ate hain — ye kisi fixed list se nahi ate, aap ke sheher ke coordinates aur tareekh se hisab hota hai. Method (Karachi waghera), Hanafi Asr aur minute adjustments **Salah Settings** mein hain.
- Status sirf chaar hain: **Waqt par, Late, Qaza, Missed** — ya phir **Unmarked** (abhi kuch nahi likha).
- Dashboard par 5 dots (nuqtay) dikhte hain — jo namaz mark ho jaye, us ka nuqta bhar jata hai. Agli namaz ka waqt aur kitni der baqi hai, wo bhi wahan likha ata hai.
- **History** mein guzishta dinon ka record hai; Reports mein haftay/mahinay ki distribution (doughnut graph) nazar ati hai.
- Ek usool: app kabhi khud se namaz ko missed nahi kehta, aur kahin koi sharminda karne wali zuban istemal nahi hoti. Record sirf aap ki apni behtari ke liye hai.

## 7. Health (Sehat)

- **Water:** quick buttons se ek click mein add karein. Roz ka total aur target ke muqable progress Dashboard par bhi dikhta hai.
- **Weight:** jab wazan karein, entry add kar dein. Har entry apni tareekh ke sath rehti hai — purani entry kabhi overwrite nahi hoti. Graph mein waqt ke sath tabdeeli nazar ati hai.
- **Walking:** distance (km), duration (minutes), chahain to steps. Haftay aur mahinay ke total Reports mein milte hain.
- **Exercise:** kisam chunein (Walking, Running, Gym, Push-ups waghera ya apni custom), duration, sets/reps agar hon.
- **Meals:** Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snack. Ek meal mein kai cheezein add ho sakti hain. Ghar ka khana ya bahir ka — ye bhi mark ho sakta hai, Reports mein is ka hisaab bhi ata hai.
- **Sleep:** **Start sleep** dabayein jab sone lagain, uth kar **End** dabayein — ya baad mein manual likh dein. Neend aadhi raat (ya subah ke auqaat) cross kare to bhi duration sahi calculate hota hai. Do neendein ek hi waqt par overlap nahi ho sakti — app rok deta hai.

## 8. Habits (Aadatein)

Aadat task se alag hai: task ek dafa ka kaam hai, aadat wo cheez hai jo aap **baar baar** kar ke apni zindagi ka hissa banana chahte hain — roz walk, roz learning, waqt par sona, freelancing par roz kaam.

Har habit card par ye nazar ata hai:
- **Habit ka naam aur maqsad** — aap ne jo description mein likha (aap ka "kyun")
- **"Done today" button** — aaj kar liya to dabayein; galti se dab gaya to dobara dabane se unmark ho jata hai
- **Streak** — lagatar kitne din se ho raha hai ("6 day streak")
- **Haftay ke 7 nuqtay (dots)** — is haftay ke har din ka haal; jo din ho gaya wo bhara hua, aj wala khaas outline ke sath. Sath likha bhi ata hai: "3 of 5 this week"
- **12 hafton ka naqsha (heatmap)** — 84 chhote khane, har khana ek din. Sabz khana = us din habit hui. Ye naqsha ek nazar mein batata hai ke consistency kesi ja rahi hai

Page ke upar **examples** bhi diye hain (Walk 30 minutes, Drink 8 glasses of water waghera) — kisi par click karne se form pehle se bhara hua khulta hai, lekin **kuch save nahi hota** jab tak aap khud Save na dabayein. Ye sirf shuruaat asaan karne ke liye hain, fake record nahi hain.

Kisi purane din ka haal dekhna ho to upar **Previous day / Next day** se tareekh badal sakte hain — aur wahan bhi us din ki tick durust kar sakte hain.

Note: streak ya heatmap ka maqsad dabao nahi hai. Ek din reh jaye to wo sirf maloomat hai — agle din se dobara shuru kar dein.

## 9. Notifications kese kaam karte hein

Notifications do tarah ke hain:

1. **In-app inbox** — app ke upar bell (ghanti) wala button. Jab koi reminder fire hota hai, wo yahan ajata hai. Baghair parhay hue (unread) notifications ki tadaad laal badge mein dikhti hai.
2. **Push notification** — phone ya laptop ki screen par pop-up, chahe app band hi kyun na ho (jab device par enable ho).

### Reminders kab fire hotay hain?
Server **har minute** check karta hai:
- Namaz ka reminder (har namaz se pehle / namaz ke waqt — Salah Settings aur Notifications settings se set hota hai)
- Task ka reminder (task mein jo "minutes before" likha tha)
- Aap ke apne **custom reminders** (masalan pani peene ya exercise ka)

Ek hi reminder **do dafa nahi** jata — system is ka record rakhta hai.

### Zaroori shartein aur limits (saaf baat)
- Push ke liye browser ki **ijazat (Allow)** zaroori hai, aur server par ek dafa **VAPID keys** set karni hoti hain (admin wala kaam, `docs/NOTIFICATIONS.md` mein tafseel hai).
- **Localhost (aap ke laptop par test):** laptop par push a jata hai — aap dekh chuke hain. Lekin **phone par push localhost se nahi ja sakta**, kyunki phone aap ke laptop ke `127.0.0.1` ko khol hi nahi sakta. Phone push ke liye app ka **live (HTTPS wala) version** chahiye hoga.
- **iPhone:** pehle Safari se app ko **Home Screen par add** karein, phir wahan se khol kar notifications enable karein (iOS 16.4 ya us se naya chahiye). Bina Home Screen par add kiye iPhone par push nahi milta — ye Apple ki pabandi hai, app ki nahi.
- Jab app kisi live server par hoga, reminders ke liye server ka **scheduler/cron** chalna zaroori hai — hosting par ye ek dafa set hota hai (deployment guide mein likha hai). Localhost par jab laptop band ho, reminders us waqt fire nahi hote; lekin in-app inbox wala record system jab chalay ga, due reminders wahan ajayenge.

### Email reminders?
Nahi — aap ke faislay ke mutabiq is app mein **email reminders nahi hain**. Sirf in-app aur push hain. (Email sirf password reset ke liye istemal hoti hai, wo bhi live server ki email setting ke sath.)

## 10. Reports aur graphs kese parhein

Reports mein Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly aur Custom (apni pasand ki tareekhein) hain. Har report mein upar ahem numbers (cards), phir graphs, phir neeche poori table hoti hai.

Graphs ka matlab:
- **Tasks graph (bar):** har din kitne tasks plan hue (halka rang) aur kitne complete hue (sabz). Sabz hissa jitna lamba, din utna acha.
- **Prayer doughnut:** mark ki gayi namazein Waqt par / Late / Qaza / Missed mein kese banti hain. Jo namaz mark hi nahi hui, wo is mein shamil nahi.
- **Expenses doughnut:** paisa kahan gaya — Food, Transport waghera categories mein.
- **Weight line:** waqt ke sath wazan ki tabdeeli. Ek do din ke utar charhao par faisla na karein — mahinay ka rukh (trend) dekhein.
- **Weekly comparison table:** is haftay ke numbers pichhle haftay ke bilkul sath — "is haftay walk pichhle haftay se zyada hui ya kam" wahan saaf nazar ata hai.
- **"What this means"** wala card kuch reports mein ajata hai — ye jumlay aap ke **asal records** se bante hain, masalan "You completed 18 of 24 planned tasks this week." Agar data kam ho to ye card nahi banta, aur khali graph ki jagah wazahat ajati hai ke pehle kya record karna hoga.

Usool yaad rakhein: **app kabhi khud se number nahi banata.** Jo graph khali hai, us ka matlab hai us arsay mein wo cheez record nahi hui — pehle record karein, graph khud ban jayega.

## 11. Baqi modules — mukhtasar

- **Office:** roz ki hazri, start/end time, break, late ana, chutti (leave). Mahinay ka summary aur salary history bhi yahin hai. Raat wali shift (end time agle din) sahi handle hoti hai.
- **Freelancing:** projects (naam, client, platform, deadline, status), aur income (gross, fees, net). Pending payments aur mahinay ki kamai Reports mein bhi ati hai.
- **Learning:** goals (maqsad), courses/skills, aur roz ke sessions (topic, minutes, notes, link). Progress percentage wahan set kar sakte hain.
- **Finance:** amdani (salary, freelancing waghera) aur kharche (Food, Transport, Bills waghera) — category, amount, tareekh, payment method. Mahinay ki amdani, kharcha aur bachat (savings) ka hisaab khud banta hai. Koi entry app **khud se nahi banata** — har entry aap ki marzi se banti hai.
- **Calendar:** mahinay ka calendar — kisi tareekh par click karein to us din ke tasks, namazein, khana, neend, exercise, finance, sab ek jagah.
- **Export / Backup:** Settings se apna data **CSV** mein download kar sakte hain, ya poora personal data export. Apna data hamesha aap ke ikhtiyar mein hai — export zaroor le kar kahin mehfooz rakhein.
- **Account delete** waghera ki settings Profile mein hain — delete se pehle export le lena behtar hai.

## 12. Aam sawal (FAQ)

**Mera data kahan mehfooz hai?**
Server ke database (MySQL) mein, aap ke account ke sath. Ek device par mark ki gayi cheez dusri device par login karne se waisi hi milti hai.

**Koi cheez galti se delete ho jaye to?**
Delete se pehle app tasdeeq (confirm) mangta hai. Phir bhi ehtiyat ke tor par kabhi kabhi Export le kar file mehfooz rakh lein.

**Theme badla tha, refresh par waisa hi raha?**
Bilkul — theme aur accent color aap ke account mein save hote hain. Dusri device par login karein ge to wahan bhi wohi theme mile ga.

**System theme kya hai?**
Is ka matlab hai app aap ke phone/computer ki apni dark/light setting ke peeche chalta hai — device dark karein, app dark; device light, app light.

**Namaz ka time meri masjid se chand minute mukhtalif hai?**
Salah Settings mein har namaz ke liye minute adjustment (+/−) diya gaya hai — wahan se barabar kar lein. Method aur Hanafi Asr apni jagah rahen ge.

**Graph khali kyun hai?**
Us arsay mein wo cheez record nahi hui. Masalan weight ka graph tab bane ga jab kam az kam ek weight entry ho. Pehle record karein, graph khud ajayega.

**Ek hi activity task, routine aur habit — teeno mein dalun?**
Nahi. Usool ye hai:
- Jo **repeat** hota hai aur waqt muqarrar hai → **Routine**
- Jo **ek dafa** ka kaam hai → **Task**
- Jo chhoti si aadat **roz tick** karke consistency dekhna chahte hain → **Habit**
Teeno apni jagah alag kaam karte hain; ek hi cheez teen jagah dalne ki zaroorat nahi.

**Phone par notification kyun nahi aya?**
Section 9 dobara dekhein — localhost par phone push mumkin nahi; live site, ijazat (permission) aur iPhone par Home Screen install zaroori hai.

## 13. Roz ka 2-minute review routine

Roz raat ko sone se pehle sirf ye paanch kaam — do minute se zyada nahi:

1. **Habits** — aj ki aadatein tick kar dein
2. **Salah** — koi namaz unmarked reh gayi ho to us ka status likh dein
3. **My Day** — jo task ho gaya wo Complete; jo nahi hua wo "Move to tomorrow" (koi guilt nahi — planning aisi hi behtar hoti hai)
4. **Sleep** — Start sleep dabayein
5. **Reports → Daily** — ek nazar poora din dekh lein

Aur **har itwaar (Sunday)** ko 10 minute: Weekly Report kholein, is haftay vs pichhle haftay ki table dekhein, aur ek hi cheez tay karein jo agle haftay behtar karni hai. Bas ek — zyada nahi.

Yahi chhota sa chakkar (plan → follow → review) is poori app ka maqsad hai.
